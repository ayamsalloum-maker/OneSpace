07 · Implementation Contract

What every future OneSpace build must obey.

The non-negotiable filter for every page, feature, and code change we implement together:

✓ Strictly Required (DO)
  • Preserve 100% of product data: Keep all existing routes, database structures, local AES-GCM vault encryption, and information banks.
  • Pure White Canvas: Maintain #FFFFFF as the base; allow color to create energy and hierarchy.
  • Give each feature its own rhythm: Choose from the 6 layout primitives instead of defaulting to identical cards.
  • Tactile feedback: Use responsive physical interactions (steppers, tilts, shuffles, reels).
  • User-owned identity: User photos and projects become covers; no fake stock illustrations.
✕ Strictly Banned (DON'T)
  • No beige or dusty washes: Never coat pages in muddy salmon or dull cream that washes out contrast.
  • No card-wall default: Before adding a card, check if an open canvas, rail, or stage works better.
  • No fake Aya/Mohamad cartoons: No clip-art avatars representing real people.
  • No excessive narration: Remove long explanatory paragraphs when an interaction is self-evident.
  • No all-circle design: Circles are reserved for avatars, progress dials, and breathing touchpoints.
